Two solo-authored papers presented at ICML workshops in 2026.

ICML Workshop 2026Solo author

The Illusion of State: Sharp Memory-Decay Bounds in Linear State Space Models

Derived asymptotically tight bounds showing that memory in linear state-space models with defective transition matrices decays as Θ(km−1ρk). Connected Jordan structure to memory retention and sequence-task behaviour.

ICML Workshop 2026Solo author

Provably Stable Neural Dynamics via Koopman Operator Certificates

Constructed stable latent dynamics with K = exp(Δt(−S + A)), where S is positive definite and A is skew-symmetric. The parameterization recorded 0% stability violations across four dynamical-systems benchmarks.
